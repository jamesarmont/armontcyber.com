Armont Aegis

macOS network defense & exposure visibility. Most tools tell you what they blocked — Aegis tells you who else is positioned to watch you, and enforces a default-deny perimeter at the kernel, in real time.

  • Live firewall & stealth — real-time kernel packet filtering with silent-drop stealth modes and auto-updated threat feeds.
  • Total visibility — every socket attributed to its process, with a Noise / Watch / Threat graph where red means a real threat.
  • Exposure mapping — detects MDM hooks, SASE/SWG agents, and trusted TLS-inspection certificates on the machine.
  • First-party by design — built entirely on Apple-native primitives; nothing it observes ever leaves your machine.

About

James Armont

Armont Cyber Security, LLC is an independent, Texas-based practice founded and run by James Armont. The model is simple: one experienced advisor, directly accountable, with no vendor allegiance.

James has spent his career at the intersection of security, networking, and enterprise communications — evaluating platforms, negotiating with providers, and guiding organizations from mid-market to household-name enterprises through technology decisions they have to live with for years.

When he isn't advising clients, he's building — Armont Aegis, the macOS network defense tool above, is designed, written, and shipped first-party by Armont Cyber.
